LeanRDF 0.5.0 gives agents four answers, each a theorem or an honest "don't know". Until now a reasoning tool could say "entailed", or "not derived", which proves nothing. Now it can also prove that a query does **not** follow, and that the data contradicts itself.

| Verdict | Exit | Backed by |
|---|---|---|
| PROVEN | 0 | a certificate the verified checker accepts |
| REFUTED | 5 | a countermodel the verified model checker accepts |
| UNKNOWN (not derived) | 1 | nothing: neither was found |
| INCONSISTENT | 4 | a clash certificate the verified checker accepts |

Scripts that read every exit code other than 0 and 2 as "not derived" must now handle 4 and 5.

## Inconsistency certificates

A graph with no model entails every query, so an ENTAILED from it carries no information. The tools now look for a clash first.

- **Clash rules**, rules with conclusion `false`, are a new rule kind: `ClashRule`. `Rule`, `Derives` and every existing soundness theorem are unchanged.
- The **twelve fixed-arity OWL 2 RL clash rules** are proven sound against the OWL conditions: `cax-dw`, `cls-nothing2`, `cls-com`, `cls-maxc1`, `cls-maxqc1`, `cls-maxqc2`, `prp-irp`, `prp-asyp`, `prp-pdw`, `prp-npa1`, `prp-npa2` and `eq-diff1`. `OWLConds` gained the ten conditions they need, and the OWL test model still satisfies all of them.
- **Theorems**: `checkClash_sound`, `checkClash_sound_owl`, `checkClash_sound_el`, `checkClash_sound_rdfs`, and the explosion `checkClash_explodes_owl`.
- `leanrdf-demo` (with the new `--profile owl`), `leanrdf-oxilite` and `leanrdf-classify` report INCONSISTENT, exit 4, before any entailment. `leanrdf-check` verifies clash certificates.
- **W3C**: 27 of the approved OWL 2 inconsistency tests are proven inconsistent with checked certificates. The other 91 are listed with the reason they are not decided, never as consistent.
- **The RDFS datatype clash**: an `xsd:string` literal containing U+0000, U+FFFE or U+FFFF. Its theorem assumes `IsWellTyped`, that ill-typed strings denote nothing, which `DInterp` does not yet force.

## Countermodels

A refutation needs a world in which the data is true and the query false. That world is infinite, so LeanRDF checks a finite **presentation** of it: sorts, a table of relations between sorts, and defaults for the infinite families of IRIs, literals and triple terms.

- **The interpretation**: `Presentation.dinterp` is a `DInterp` over a free carrier, so `IT` and the literal values are injective by construction.
- **Lifting lemmas**: every RDF and RDFS condition has a finite form over the sorts, and a lemma proves that it makes the condition hold on every element (`isRDF_of`, `isRDFS_of`).
- **The model checker**: `checkCountermodel` checks well-formedness, the regime's conditions, the data, and every sort assignment of the query's blank nodes, within a step budget.
- **Theorems**: `checkCountermodel_sound_simple`, `_rdf` and `_rdfs` give `¬ g.SimpleEntails q`, `¬ g.RDFEntails q` and `¬ g.RDFSEntails q`. `checkCountermodel_consistent` proves the data has an RDFS model, so REFUTED and INCONSISTENT never coincide.
- **The finder** (in `leanrdf-elpi`) builds the canonical model of the Elpi closure. Where the rules are not complete for the data, the checker rejects the candidate, and the verdict stays NOT DERIVED with the failed condition.
- **Tools**: `leanrdf-demo` reports REFUTED (exit 5) and writes the countermodel with `--countermodel`. `leanrdf-check --countermodel FILE` verifies it. The JSON verdict is `refuted`, with the theorem and the datatypes.
- **W3C**: no positive test of the RDF 1.2 semantics suite is ever refuted. One of the four negative tests is refuted; the other three need triple terms in different sorts, and are reported unknown.

## What is proven and what is trusted

- Every theorem above is checked by Lean's kernel, with only `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`.
- **REFUTED is relative to D** = {xsd:string, rdf:langString, rdf:dirLangString}. Under a larger D, an ill-typed literal could make the data unsatisfiable.
- **Scope**: countermodels exist for simple, RDF and RDFS entailment. OWL countermodels are not implemented. All triple terms share one sort.
- **Trust**: the finders and searches are untrusted. `native_decide` in tests trusts the Lean compiler, as before.
